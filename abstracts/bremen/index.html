Nonlinear Dynamics of Microbubbles in Tissue

Nov 21, 2024·
David Sinden

High-intensity focused ultrasound (HIFU) aims to destroy pathogenic tissue by converting energy from ultrasound into heat. At certain pressures and frequencies, cavitation activity has been shown to greatly enhance the local rate of heat deposition and also provide a significant aid to treatment monitoring. However, cavitation occurring pre-focally or growing unstably around the focal region is known to impede safe and effective treatment delivery. Thus, the influence of cavitation on wave propagation and beam focusing and thus effective treatment delivery is of real importance. This talk presents the rich nonlinear behaviour the bubbles, stable and chaotic oscillation, interactions, and strategies to control their oscillations.
