Artificial Intelligence in Therapeutic Ultrasound

Apr 20, 2023·
David Sinden
,
Sven Rothlübbers
· 1 min read

This educational talk presents an accessible introduction to the basic methods and terminology in artificial intelligence which could be applied to develop tools for therapeutic ultrasound delivery. The talk begins with an introduction to AI, focusing on what different neural networks are, and what is necessary to train a network. With this knowledge, a small number of applications are presented, covering segmentation and registration, treatment planning and physics-informed neural networks, treatment monitoring and outcome prediction.

Senior Research Scientist

David Sinden is an applied mathematician working as a senior scientist at the Fraunhofer Institute for Digital Medicine MEVIS, in Bremen.

His research interests include ultrasound modelling, thermal ablation and pharmacokinetic models. He works within the modelling and simulation and image-guided therapy groups.
